# timeline_visualizer

Upload a Google Maps Timeline export and see your trips on a map — filtered by transport mode, played back over time, and summarised by an LLM that never sees your coordinates.

> Part of [ai-apps](https://github.com/manish-lad1/ai-apps) — a portfolio of small, self-contained AI and product engineering projects.
