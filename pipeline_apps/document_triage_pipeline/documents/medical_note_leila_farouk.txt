REASON FOR REFERRAL
Management referral following an absence of six weeks. Advice requested
on fitness to return and on reasonable adjustments.

BACKGROUND
The employee reports a twelve month history of migraine with aura,
increasing to eight episodes per month at its worst. Under the care of a
neurologist since 2026-01. Started on propranolol 80 mg daily as
prophylaxis, with sumatriptan 50 mg as required for acute attacks.

CURRENT POSITION
Episodes have reduced to approximately two per month. The employee feels
able to return to work and is keen to do so.

OPINION
The employee is fit to return to work with the adjustments below. The
underlying condition is likely to meet the definition of a disability
under the Equality Act 2010, and I would advise the employer to proceed
on that basis.

RECOMMENDED ADJUSTMENTS
1. Phased return over four weeks, starting at three days per week.
2. Screen glare filter and a workstation away from direct overhead light.
3. Permission to take unscheduled breaks in a quiet space during an
   episode.
4. Flexibility on start time following a night time attack.

REVIEW
Suggest a further review in three months, or sooner if the frequency of
episodes increases.
