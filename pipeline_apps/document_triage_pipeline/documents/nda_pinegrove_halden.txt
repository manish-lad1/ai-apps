MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Agreement reference: NDA-PG-2026-007
Effective date:      2026-01-30

BETWEEN:
  (1) Pinegrove Labs Ltd, registered in England and Wales,
      company number 11235813; and
  (2) Halden Robotics Ltd, registered in England and Wales,
      company number 07781004.

1. PURPOSE
   The parties wish to explore a potential collaboration on machine
   vision systems for warehouse automation and will exchange confidential
   information for that purpose only.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
   Any information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form, that
   is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to
   be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

3. OBLIGATIONS
   3.1 Each party shall use the other's confidential information solely
       for the purpose set out in clause 1.
   3.2 Neither party shall disclose the other's confidential information
       to any third party without prior written consent.
   3.3 Each party shall protect the other's confidential information with
       no less care than it applies to its own.

4. EXCLUSIONS
   Clause 3 does not apply to information that is or becomes public
   through no fault of the receiving party, was already lawfully known to
   the receiving party, or is required to be disclosed by law.

5. TERM
   This Agreement remains in force for three years from the effective
   date. The obligations in clause 3 survive expiry for a further
   two years.

6. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales and the
   parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.
